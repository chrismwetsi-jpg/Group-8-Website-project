Privacy Guidelines

Privacy and Reporting Guidance

Please read these guidelines before submitting a waste collection report or uploading photographs.

1. What Information Is Collected

We only collect the details necessary to identify and verify a waste issue. This includes your parish or specific location, the issue category, a short description, and an optional photograph. You do not need to create an account or submit sensitive personal identification.

2. How Reports Are Reviewed

Every submitted report is reviewed by a site administrator before any action is taken. The administrator confirms that the location falls within active pilot boundaries and checks uploaded photos for authenticity before sharing the details with licensed collectors or local council leaders.

3. Photo Guidance

When attaching a photograph, frame only the waste pile, overflowing bin, or blocked drainage point. Avoid capturing identifiable faces of residents, children, or private vehicle number plates.

4. Limits of the Service

Kampala Clean Neighborhood is a community information and reporting platform. Submitting a report helps highlight recurring issues, but it does not replace KCCA operations or guarantee immediate waste pickup.
