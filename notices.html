Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Does submitting a report guarantee immediate garbage collection?

No. Our platform helps log recurring issues and shares verified reports with local leaders and collectors, but it does not replace KCCA operations.

How do I know if a waste collector is licensed?

Only collectors verified with KCCA and local authorities are listed on our Find a Waste Collector directory.

Where should I put my rubbish if I miss the truck?

Keep waste stored safely inside your compound until the next scheduled collection day. Never dump rubbish in drainage channels or along roadsides.
